Upstream sources are unmodified and compiled as the separate libmp3lame.so
shared library. The reproducible Android build and JNI adapter are in
app/src/main/cpp/CMakeLists.txt and mp3_encoder.cpp. All included ABIs are
compiled from source with 16 KiB ELF page alignment.

The previous jump3r Java implementation is retained only in instrumented
benchmark tests (LGPL 2.1 or later), and is not included in the app installer.
Source: https://git.iem.at/sciss/jump3r
